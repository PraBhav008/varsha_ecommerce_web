About Varsha Furniture

Direct Manufacturing. Uncompromising Detail.

Varsha Furniture operates directly out of Shraddha Industrial Hub in Singarwa, Ahmedabad. We specialize in precision-engineered office seating and contemporary dining furniture tailored for homes, corporate offices, and hospitality venues.

2. Pricing & Quotations

Prices shown in the catalog are in Indian Rupees (INR) and are indicative workshop prices. Final invoices for bulk, institutional, or custom upholstery orders are issued upon order confirmation.

Standard products in regular inventory are dispatched within 2 to 4 business days. Custom finishes, large commercial suites, or tailored upholstery selections typically require 7 to 14 days manufacturing time.
